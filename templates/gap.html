HOW THIS SCORE WORKS

Clear math. No random scores.

For each required skill, we divide your level by its target level, cap the result at 100%, then average the skill scores. Missing skills score 0. Beginner, Intermediate, and Advanced are levels 1, 2, and 3.

Readiness = average( min(your level ÷ target level, 1) × 100 )
